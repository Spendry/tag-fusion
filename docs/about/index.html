About TagFusion

A human-scale way to find the web.

What is TagFusion?

TagFusion is a public directory for discovering independent, creative, educational, and playful websites. Visitors browse broad categories, combine descriptive tags, read a short explanation, and then decide whether a destination deserves a closer look. The goal is to make it easier to follow an interest into a neighboring one, not to replace a general-purpose search engine.

Who runs it?

TagFusion is run by one person, Spendry. That person picks the listings, writes the descriptions, keeps the tags, and reads every suggestion. This is practical experience with this directory, not a claim of expertise in every subject its listings cover.

How are suggestions reviewed?

Anyone can suggest a site with its name, address, a short description, a category, and up to four tags. Suggestions are public and are read before anything is listed. A site is listed when it fits the directory, has a clear purpose, and its link works.

Editorial policy

TagFusion is a discovery directory, not a safety check or expert review service. Categories and tags describe what a site offers; they do not verify every claim on that site.

TagFusion does not list sites whose main content is adult material, gambling, the sale or dosing of drugs, piracy, malware, scams, or hate. Some listings are platforms where other people publish their own work, or directories that link onward; those are doors, not vetted pages, and what lies behind them is not reviewed.

Every listing is re-checked on a schedule, because web addresses change hands. A listing is removed when its address stops working, starts leading somewhere else, or no longer fits. In November 2026 nine listings were removed in such a check.

There are no paid placements today. If one is ever shown, it will be labelled “Sponsor” and kept apart from the listings.

How should visitors use a listing?

Treat each result as a starting point. Check who runs the site and verify important claims with original sources. TagFusion does not guarantee that an external site is current, accurate, secure, accessible, or right for a particular decision.

If a listing is broken or out of date, report it or use the contact page.

TagFusion. Made for curious people, not perfect queries.
