Privacy

A clear view of your data.

TagFusion is operated by one person. This page says what the site does with information about you. The short answer is: almost nothing.

What this site collects

Nothing. TagFusion has no accounts, no sign-in, no forms that store what you type, no cookies, and no analytics. Searching and combining tags happen inside your own browser.

Who else sees your visit

  • GitHub Pages hosts the site. Like any web host, GitHub receives your IP address and browser details when a page loads, under GitHub’s privacy statement.
  • Google Fonts serves the typefaces, so Google receives the same kind of request when the fonts load.

Suggestions and reports

Suggesting or reporting a site happens on GitHub, in public, under your GitHub account. If you email instead, your address is used only to answer you.

Links to other websites

Listings link to independent websites. Those destinations have their own privacy practices. TagFusion does not control their content, cookies, or data handling.

Earlier accounts

Until late 2026 TagFusion ran on a different platform and offered accounts. Those accounts and the data stored with them were deleted when the site moved.
